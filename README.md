
# AI Agent Adoption & Workplace Productivity Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard designed to explore AI agent adoption and its relationship with workplace productivity, employee satisfaction, training, operational costs, and job risk across different industries and years.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Records
- Average Hours Saved per Week
- Average Error Rate
- Average Job Satisfaction
- Average Monthly AI Cost
- Total Training Hours
- Average Productivity Change

## Dashboard Visualizations
1. AI Agent Adoption Distribution
2. Productivity Change by Industry
3. Overall AI Impact Distribution
4. Average Hours Saved by Year
5. Training Hours vs. Productivity Change
6. Job Satisfaction by AI Usage
7. Job Risk by AI Usage Level

## Interactive Filters
- AI Agent Usage
- Year
- Country
- Industry
- Job Role

## Tools & Skills
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- KPI design and dashboard layout
- Interactive slicers and filtering
- Data analysis and visualization
- Business insight communication

## Key Objective
To make it easier to explore patterns in AI adoption and compare productivity, satisfaction, training, costs, and job risk across different business segments.
